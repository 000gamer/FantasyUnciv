# FantasyUnciv

### Build your civilization in a world of magic and steel!

[简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) · **Version 2.0**

**FantasyUnciv** is a complete fantasy ruleset mod for Unciv: an original world, twelve civilizations, several victory paths that all work, and things the base game does not have, such as cities at sea, boss city-states and heroes.

## Orbis: an original fantasy world

The game is set in **Orbis** (万方), a world where nature and magic exist side by side. It is not a copy of any existing setting: the races, civilizations, history and wonders were written for this mod.

- **Six races, twelve civilizations, each on its own path.** Human Empire, Mercantile Republic, Explorers Guild; Forest Elves, Sea Elves; Mountain Dwarves, Artificer Dwarves; Nomadic Orcs, Orc Empire; Industrial Goblins; Lich Dynasty, Bone Empire. Forest realms, nomads, merchant cities and magitech industrial states share the same world.
- **No ultimate evil that everyone must unite against.** Conflict comes from land and resources, tradition and technology, nature and industry. The undead are not evil by default: the two undead civilizations each have their own social tradition.
- **Machines first, magitech after.** Armies advance from spears and crossbows to firearms, and then magic is joined to machinery: magitech cannons, constructs and airships. Eight eras, 80 technologies and 10 policy branches follow this line.
- **Magic is part of nature, but it is not all-powerful.** It cannot create things from nothing or affect time. Whether gods exist, and what follows death, remain open questions.

## Several ways to win, no single right answer

The balance goal is that every victory type is within reach and none of them is the only sensible choice.

| Victory | How to win |
|---|---|
| Magic | Complete the Gate Beyond Project, build its six parts in six different cities and bring them to your capital |
| Cultural | Complete 5 policy branches and build the Utopia Project |
| Religious | Build the Grand Synod and make your faith the world religion |
| Diplomatic | Win the vote of civilizations and city-states once the Assembly of Nations is built |
| Domination | Capture every capital |
| Time | Have the highest score when the turns run out |

We check this with automated all-AI games. In the latest round, all 26 games (Medium, Large and Huge maps, 6 to 12 civilizations) produced a winner, after about 330 turns on average:

| Religious | Magic | Cultural | Diplomatic | Domination |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 games | 7 games | 5 games | 5 games | 1 game |

The four peaceful paths win at similar rates. The AI rarely wins by conquest, so that path is mostly the player's: late-game cities defend better and ranged and siege fire is more restrained, so taking every capital takes a well-run army.

## What the base game does not have

**Cities at sea.** Sea Elves can train Reef Settlers, raise a reef island from suitable shallow water and found a city on it, extending their realm beyond the shoreline.

**Boss city-states.** The Dragon Lair and the Giant Fortress are two heavily defended city-states, guarded by an Elder Dragon, Wyrmlings and giants. Take them to win Dragon Scale and Giant Hearts, and forge Dragonscale Halberds and Giant Hammers; the later the era, the richer the spoils.

**Heroes.** 12 heroes, each with a personal promotion path, such as the army-leading Oathsworn Commander, the forest-defending Guardian of the Wilds, the arcane Star-Gazer Sage and the Ancient Oath Dragon. A hero is won by completing a wonder, and only one of each exists in the world.

**The Gate Beyond.** The magic victory is not a spaceship but a gate to another world: its six parts must be built in six different cities and escorted to the capital.

**Ruins, wonders beneath the sea, and city-states.** 19 ruin outcomes (4 of them ask you to choose), 19 natural wonders (some, like the Sunken Bell Reef and the Whalefall Trench, can only be found from the sea), and 16 regular city-states, each with its own allied bonus and its own events.

## Begin your journey

1. Install through Unciv’s mod manager using this repository’s GitHub URL, or place the repository contents in `mods/FantasyUnciv/`.
2. When creating a game, select **FantasyUnciv** as the base ruleset.

## Contributions welcome

Contributions are welcome: player feedback, balance suggestions, translations, code, new civilizations and stories. Share your discoveries in an issue or contribute through a pull request.

Artists are welcome to create civilization emblems, units, terrain, buildings and hero designs!
