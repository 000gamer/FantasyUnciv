# FantasyUnciv

### Build your civilization in a world of magic and steel!

[简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) · **Version 2.0**

**FantasyUnciv** is a complete fantasy ruleset mod for Unciv: a new world, mechanics, civilizations, technologies, units, buildings and wonders!

## An original fantasy world — Orbis

**Orbis is a world where nature and magic coexist.** Humans, elves, dwarves, orcs, goblins and the undead: six races and twelve civilizations shape its history and conflicts.

Lead your civilization through **eight eras**: from early settlements to city-states and kingdoms, through ocean exploration and industrial transformation, and into the Magitech and Emergent Eras. From spears and crossbows to firearms and magitech, experience the progress of civilization driven by technology and magic together.

## Balanced gameplay

**Every path to victory should be worth pursuing, and every civilization should have room to shine.** We carefully tune mechanics and numbers to keep any one path or civilization from gaining an overwhelming advantage. AI simulation games have shown a broadly balanced distribution of victory types, giving different playstyles a chance to win.

## Distinctive city-states

**Ally with city-states to gain unique bonuses!** City-states differ in more than just their names: each offers its own pact rewards. Choose allies to suit your strategy, and give every friendship a distinct strategic value.

## Build cities on the sea

**Sea Elves can build cities on the sea!** Train Reef Settlers to raise islands in suitable shallow waters, found cities on them, and extend your civilization beyond the shoreline.

## Summon heroes

**Compete for hero wonders and recruit one-of-a-kind heroes.** Each hero has unique abilities and dedicated promotions, bringing new tactical options to your army and helping you write your own legend on the battlefield.

## Challenge bosses

**Challenge powerful bosses and claim unique rewards!** The Dragon Lair and the Giant Fortress occupy the map as special city-states with formidable defenders. Muster your army, defeat these mighty foes, and claim the spoils.

## Explore distant lands

**Every expedition could begin a new story.** With 15 regular ruin outcomes, four land ruin events and five ocean wonder events, exploration offers a wealth of discoveries and choices. Enter ancient ruins, sail into uncharted waters, and make decisions in story events to uncover the past and earn rewards.

## Begin your journey

1. Install through Unciv’s mod manager using this repository’s GitHub URL, or place the repository contents in `mods/FantasyUnciv/`.
2. When creating a game, select **FantasyUnciv** as the base ruleset.

## Contributions welcome

We welcome player feedback, balance suggestions, translations, code, and new civilizations and stories. Share your discoveries in an issue or contribute through a pull request.

Artists are welcome to help create civilization emblems, units, terrain, buildings and hero designs!
